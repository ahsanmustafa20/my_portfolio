🚧 Final Year Project — In Progress

Bilingual Sign Language Recognition System (ASL & PSL)

A bilingual AI-powered sign language recognition platform designed to improve communication accessibility for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals. The system supports both American Sign Language (ASL) and Pakistan Sign Language (PSL). The ASL module recognizes complete words and phrases and utilizes Gemini AI to generate meaningful sentences. The PSL module focuses on real-time alphabet recognition using Computer Vision and Deep Learning techniques. Through real-time webcam processing, the platform converts sign language into readable text and helps bridge communication gaps between sign language users and non-signers.
